- Suppose we charge one conductor with excess charge, and then connect it with a wire to another, uncharged conductor.
- What happens & why? 
- → Electric field will cause Coulomb force on charges and make them move through the wire until the conductor is discharged. 
- So we know that the wire has *charge moving* through it – this is what we call a *current*. Can run motor, light bulb, …
