- Corrects cardiac arrhythmia by applying a pulse of electrical energy across the heart.
- Can be life-saving.
- Portable defibrillators are now common
- Easily used by non-experts.

![[Pasted image 20240127154437.png]]

- A capacitor is charged to a few hundred volts 
- The stored energy is released quickly across the patient’s chest. 
- Aimed to restart the normal cardiac rhythm.
