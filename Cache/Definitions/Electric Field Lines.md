- A charge or a distribution of charges produces an the electric field at every point in space.
- The electric field is a vector.
- This vector defined the magnitude and direction of the electric field at each point in space.
- We can visualize the electric field by drawing the *E* vectors at several points.
![[Pasted image 20240111122154.png]]
- Alternatively, we can visualize an electric field using electric field lines.
- Electric field lines point in the direction of the electric field vector.

**The density of field lines (in 3D) is proportional to the magnitude of the electric field**

![[Pasted image 20240111122632.png]]

Electric field lines are useful for visualizing the electric field. The electric field lines are constructed such that:
 - The electric field at any point is tangent to the field line.
 - The density of the lines is proportional to the magnitude of the electric field.

Rules for constructing electric field lines:
- Lines begin on a positive charge and end on a negative charge.
- The number of lines beginning on a positive charge or terminating on a negative charge is proportional to the magnitude of charge.
- No two field lines can cross.
- Field lines can start or end at infinity.
