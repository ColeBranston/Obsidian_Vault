- In metals: charge carriers move freely. 
- *Without electric field:* random motions at high velocity (thermal), $v_e$ . 
- Electrons constantly collide with electrons and ions. For copper at room temperature: typically about 1014 collisions per second! 
	- Time between collisions $(\tau ): \tau_{Cu} \text{ approx. } 10^{-14}$ 𝑠𝑒𝑐!!
- Collisions randomize motions and make average velocity (drift velocity) zero. Thus: *no current.*
