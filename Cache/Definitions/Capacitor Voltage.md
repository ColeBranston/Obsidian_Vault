
- Surface charge density on each plate is:

$$\sigma =\frac{Q}{A}$$
- This produces an electric field between the plates of:
$$E=4\pi k\sigma = \frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0} = \frac{Q}{\epsilon_0 A}$$
	- recall: permittivity of free space is 𝜀0 = 1/(4𝜋k) = 8.85×10–12 C2/Nm2 [or F/m]
- We showed previously that the potential difference V between the plates is:

$$V=Ed$$

So:

$$V=\frac{Qd}{\epsilon_0 A}$$
