**Electric Dipole in an Electric Field**

 - What happens when a dipole is placed in an electric field
 ![[Pasted image 20240112093433.png]]
- Recall: field lines originate on positive charges and end on negative charges.

- Imagine an electric dipole in an electric field as shown.
- The force on the − charge acts to the left, while the force on the + charge acts to the right.
- This produces a torque on the dipole that tends to align it with the field.
 ![[Pasted image 20240112093531.png]]
- For charges ±q separated by a distance d, we define the dipole moment vector 𝑝⃗ to have magnitude p = qd and to point from the − charge to the + charge.
- Then the torque is $\tau = p*E = pEsin\theta = (qE)(dsin\theta)$
