# Current & Charge Carriers

- In most conductors: electrons. They move in the direction opposite to the current. 
- In some materials (e.g. semiconductors): charge carriers are positive. 
- In electrolytes, plasmas, semiconductors you can have both positive and negative carriers. 
- All charge carriers, + and - contribute to the total current!
