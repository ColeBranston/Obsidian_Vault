# Work and Potential Energy

The work done by the electric field is:

$$= -(\frac{kqq_0}{r_B} - \frac{kqq_0}{r_A})$$

But the work is also related to the difference in electric potential energy, 

$$W = -(U_B - U_A)$$

Comparing these expressions, we see that the potential energy of a system of two point charges q and q0 separated by a distance r is given by:

$$U(r) = \frac{kqq_0}{r}$$

**Work done by the electric field depends only on the change in distance**

# Electric Potential Energy Summary

The electric potential energy of a system of two point charges is:

$$U(r) = \frac{kqq_0}{r}$$

**Things to notice**: 

- U(r) is a scalar, not a vector. Because of this, it is sometimes easier to work with potential energy than with [[Electric Force]] or electric field. 
- U(r) = 0 if the separation r of the charges is infinite. 
- U(r) > 0 if the two charges are of the same sign.
- U(r) < 0 if the two charges are of opposite signs.


# Work done by vs. against the electric field

Electric potential between two point charged is $U(r) \frac{kqq_0}{r}$, **U(r) > 0 if the two charges are of the same sign.** 

# Same Sign

- the distance between two particles are inversely proportional to the electric potential energy between the two particles. $\therefore$ - Distance → +PE
- Electric fields do **negative** [[Work]] when like charges are brought closer together. 
- Alternatively, we do **positive** work against the electric field to bring like charges together.
- If we move the charges further apart...
	- That is, the electric field does positive work when like charges are moved further apart.
	- We do negative [[Work]] against the electric field when like charges are moved further apart.

# Opposite Signs

- The electric field does positive work when opposite charges are brought closer together.
- We do negative [[Work]] against the electric field when opposite charges are brought closer together.
- The electric field does negative [[Work]] when opposite charges are moved further apart.
- We do positive work against the electric field when opposite charges are moved further apart.
