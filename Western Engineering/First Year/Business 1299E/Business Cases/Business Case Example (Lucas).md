
# Role & Decision

We are **Jada Abimbola** CEO of workplace safety trainers (WST)

Abimbola is looking to develop a strategy for WST after experiencing stagnating profits during the COVID-19 pandemic. 

**These Include:**

1. Offering First Aid Training
2. Developing and selling a new line of physical goods
3. Sell the business and moving on.

$\therefore$ Alternatives are 
	→ Status Quo
	→ First Aid Training
	→ Developing and selling a new line of physical goods
	→ Sell the business

# Want Analysis

*Personal:* Wants to increase value of WST so she can sell it. Wants to pursue other ventures.
→ Sell the business allows her to pursue other business ventures
→ Status quo does not change potential profitability and she has to remain as CEO

*Professional:* Wants WST to be Ontario's largest training supplier. Employ employee-centered strategy:
→ First-aid training can help expand customer base and not turn away customers.
→ Logbooks can help strategy since it is not as taxing for employees

*Risk Tolerance:*

|---------------------------------------------------------X-------------------|
Low                                                                                                     High

*Explanation:* Has bought companies before and sold them. Is losing to do this with WST, which takes a high risk tolerance.
→ Status Quo does not capitalize on her high risk tolerance
→ First aid training is more in line since it is a new course WST has not does before, not developed.
