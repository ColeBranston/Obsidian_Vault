
A general manager must determine how to benefit from opportunities while keeping in mind the company's scare resources.

- You must learn to manage resources in order to come up with a general business strategy.

# Framework

1. **Analysis**
<br>
	**Want**: **Title → Personal Wants (Case Facts) → Implications (Connection(s) to specific alternatives**
		- Personal Preferences
			- Management personal goals and objectives (short term & long-term)
				- eg. family obligation, retirement, passions...etc.
		- Professional Preferences
			- management goals and objectives for the business (short term & long term)
				- eg. growth, increased profitability, more customers..etc.
		- Risk Tolerance
			- Risk Spectrum
				- Based on your understanding of the owner(s) of the business
		**Marks given for implications for alternatives and actions plan**
<br>
	**Need** 
		- Environment
			- PEST Analysis 
				- Opportunities & Threats
				- (KSF's)
		- Consumer
			- Segments
				- Opportunities 
				- (KSF's)
		- Trade/Supply Chain
			- Roles & Demands of suppliers
			- Power?
				- Opportunities & Threats
				- (KSF's)
		- Competition
			- Who are they? (Strengths & Weaknesses)
				- Opportunities & Threats
				- (KSF's)
		*Key Success Factors:*
			*- Recipe (& ingredients) for success*
			*- What does it take to reach success?*
			*- Applies to all firms in industry*
			<br>
		List the KSF's...
		- Generated based on key insights from PEST, Consumer, Competitor, Trade Chain
			3/4 KSF's
	**Can**
		- Finance
		- Marketing
		- Operations
		- Human Resources
		-> Internal Implications
			-> Do the firm's capabilities FIT with the key success factors.

Key Success Factors
	- Does the firm have what it takes to deliver on the KSF's
	- Steps to follow...
		- Place a check or an x beside each KSF & briefly explain WHY.
		- Draw implications to explain how the business can maintain or obtain the KSF's

**How to achieve success**
- Find the best "fit" with the decision:
	- Need
	- Want
	- Can

2. **Evaluation of Alternatives**
	- Quantitative (Financial Feasibility & discuss members)
		- Use your general management math tool kit:
			- Breakeven
			- Net benefit & Payback, NPV
			- Ratios
			- Projected statement of earnings
		- Firm Evaluation
			- Net Book Value (NBV=Assets-Liabilities & Interpret Results)
			- Economic Appraisal (Fair market value of assets - Liabilities & Interpret results)
			- Overall Discussion (What is the business worth overall? Why? & Who would buy it?)
			- Capitalization of Earnings (Projected net profit after tax x P/E Multiple & Discuss rationale and interpret results)
		-> financial tool is used based on the information given
	- Qualitative (pros & cons)

3. Decision
	- Decision Upfront (& specific recommendations)
	- Qualitative Support
	- Quantitative Support
	- Why not the alternatives?
	- Risks & Mitigation
	- Conclusion
-> Must need to convince
