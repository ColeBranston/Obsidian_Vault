**Peer-to-peer (P2P)** is an architecture where end systems communicate directly, each both requesting and providing service, with no always-on server in the middle.

- **Self-scaling:** each arriving peer brings demand *and* upload capacity. What slows client–server makes P2P faster. The origin uploads once and peers pass it on.
- **Harder in every other way:** peers come and go without warning, sit behind home routers that refuse incoming connections, change addresses, and owe nobody anything.
