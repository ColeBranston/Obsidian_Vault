The **participation constraint** on a specialization/generalization determines whether every member of the [[Superclass]] must also be a member of a [[Subclass]].

- **Mandatory** — every superclass member belongs to at least one subclass.
- **Optional** — a superclass member may belong to no subclass at all.
- Written first in the UML constraint label, e.g. `{Mandatory, Or}`. Pairs with the [[Disjoint Constraint]] to give four categories.
