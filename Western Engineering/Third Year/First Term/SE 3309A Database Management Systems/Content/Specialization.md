**Specialization** is the process of *maximizing the differences* between members of an entity by identifying their distinguishing characteristics — a top-down way of splitting a [[Superclass]] into [[Subclass]]es.

- The reverse direction of [[Generalization]]; both end up at the same kind of superclass/subclass hierarchy.
- e.g. noticing that managers, sales personnel, and secretaries each need attributes the others don't, and splitting them out of `Staff`.
