The **disjoint constraint** describes the relationship between members of the subclasses: whether a member of the [[Superclass]] can belong to only one [[Subclass]], or to more than one.

- **Disjoint (`Or`)** — a member belongs to at most one subclass.
- **Nondisjoint (`And`)** — a member can belong to several subclasses at once (overlapping).
- Written second in the UML constraint label, e.g. `{Optional, Or}`. Pairs with the [[Participation Constraint]] to give four categories.
