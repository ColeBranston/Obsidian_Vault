A **superclass** is an [[Entity Type]] whose occurrences include one or more distinct subgroupings, each modelled as a [[Subclass]].

- Holds the attributes and relationships common to all its members; subclasses get them through [[Attribute Inheritance]].
- Not every superclass member has to belong to a subclass — whether it must is set by the [[Participation Constraint]].
- Its subclasses may be **distinct** or **overlapping** — set by the [[Disjoint Constraint]].
- UML notation: a hollow triangle on the superclass end of the lines coming up from its subclasses.
