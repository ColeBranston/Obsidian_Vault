A **subclass** is a distinct subgrouping of the occurrences of an [[Entity Type]] (its [[Superclass]]).

- The superclass/subclass relationship is **one-to-one (1:1)** — a subclass occurrence and its superclass occurrence are the same real-world object.
- Every member of a subclass is also a member of the superclass.
- A subclass can have its own attributes *and* its own relationships (e.g. only `Manager` manages a `Branch`) on top of what it inherits.
