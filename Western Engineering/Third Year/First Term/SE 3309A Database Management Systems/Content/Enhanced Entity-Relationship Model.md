The **Enhanced Entity-Relationship (EER) model** is the basic ER model extended with extra "semantic" modelling concepts — mainly [[Specialization]] / [[Generalization]], [[Aggregation]], and [[Composition]].

- **Why it exists:** newer database applications have more demanding requirements, and basic ER concepts aren't expressive enough to capture them.
- In this course, EER diagrams are drawn using **UML** notation.
