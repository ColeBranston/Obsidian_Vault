The **Enhanced Entity-Relationship (EER) model** is the original ER model extended with extra *semantic* modeling concepts. It exists because newer database applications have requirements that basic ER concepts ([[Entity Type]], [[Relationship Type]], [[Attribute]]) can't represent well.

The three most useful additions:
- [[Specialization and Generalization]] (built on [[Superclass and Subclass]])
- [[Aggregation]]
- [[Composition]]

This course draws all three in **UML notation**, like the rest of its ER diagrams.
