An **alphabet** Σ is a [[Set]] of symbols. Any set of symbols will do.

- Σ = {0, 1} — the **binary alphabet**
- Σ = {A, B, C … X, Y, Z} — the 26 upper-case Latin letters

**Powers of an alphabet:** Σᵏ is the set of [[String]]s of length exactly k. For Σ = {0, 1}:

| | |
|---|---|
| Σ⁰ | {ε} |
| Σ¹ | {0, 1} |
| Σ² | {00, 01, 10, 11} — all 2² = 4 strings of length 2 |

Taking the union of every power gives [[Kleene Star]], Σ*.

An alphabet is the first of the five components of a [[Deterministic Finite Automaton]], and the thing a [[Formal Language]] is defined "over".
