**Stakeholder requirements** express the needs of a specific stakeholder or group of stakeholders — customers, users, or suppliers.

They communicate stakeholders' material interest in the outcome of the product, service, or result, and provide the basis for identifying [[Solution Requirement|solution requirements]].
