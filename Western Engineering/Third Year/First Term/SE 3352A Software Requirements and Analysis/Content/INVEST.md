**INVEST** is a checklist for judging the quality of a [[User Story]]:

| Letter | Criterion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Independent | self-contained, so it can be released without depending on other stories |
| **N** | Negotiable | captures only the essence of the user's need and leaves room for conversation — not a contract |
| **V** | Valuable | delivers value to the end user |
| **E** | Estimable | can be estimated, so it can be prioritized and fitted into sprints |
| **S** | Small | small enough to finish in about 3–4 days |
| **T** | Testable | confirmable through pre-written acceptance criteria (its [[Definition of Done]]) |
