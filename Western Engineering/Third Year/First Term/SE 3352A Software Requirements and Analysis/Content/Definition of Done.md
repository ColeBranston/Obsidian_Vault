The **definition of done** (acceptance test) is the list of conditions a [[User Story]] must meet to count as complete. It's used to guide the testing process, and it's what makes a story **Testable** under [[INVEST]].

Example — for *"As a site member, I can subscribe to an RSS feed of news…"*, done when the user:
- can create a member account
- can use the account to log in
- can subscribe to an RSS feed
- can receive the feed

> [!tip] Notice the hidden prerequisites
> The story only mentions subscribing, but "done" also requires an account and a login — writing the definition of done surfaces those dependencies.
