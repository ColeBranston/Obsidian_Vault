Marlene Caroselli's *The Critical Thinking Toolkit* splits critical thinking into three categories:

| Category | Style | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Analytical thinking** | linear, logical | analyze **what is there**. "Critical" comes from the Greek *krisis*, "to separate" (see [[Analytical Skill]]). |
| **Creative thinking** | non-linear | contemplate **what is not there** — non-traditional thinking for non-typical situations. Jonathan Swift called vision "the art of seeing the invisible." |
| **Quick thinking** | fast | speed is necessary but **not sufficient** — it must be backed by analytical or creative thought (or both); hasty reactions without deliberate thought can be disastrous personally and for organizations. |
