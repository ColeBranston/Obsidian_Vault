*BABOK v3* groups requirements into four categories: [[Business Requirement]], [[Stakeholder Requirement]], [[Solution Requirement]], and [[Transition Requirement]].

> [!note]- Terms vary by source
> These four categories come from BABOK v3. Other resources may use different terms or divisions — what matters is the general aspects to look for during requirements elicitation, regardless of the specific terms used.

> [!note]- PMI's list (Section 5 reading)
> The PMI practice guide uses the same business / stakeholder / solution (functional + non-functional) / transition categories, and adds three more:
> - **Project requirements** — actions, processes and conditions the project itself must satisfy
> - **Quality requirements** — criteria for completing deliverables and showing compliance with standards and quality metrics
> - **Program requirements** — what's needed to deliver the program's benefits
>
> These three are part of the project/program work (owned by the PM), **not** the requirements process.
