A **requirements glossary** lists and defines the terms used on a project, to **limit the vocabulary and reduce ambiguity**.

- **Business-specific terms** — if the business uses a word in a special sense, list and define it. E.g. at a car agency "Car" may mean one specific type of vehicle, as opposed to "van", "crossover" or "truck" · InstaCart distinguishes "Driver" from "Shopper".
- **Synonyms** — if several terms are used interchangeably for the same concept, link them and try to settle on one. E.g. in an e-commerce app, customer · client · shopper · merchant may all refer to the same concept.

> [!note] CoreX takes this further
> [[CoreX]] calls this a **controlled vocabulary**: each word must mean the same thing everywhere it appears, and synonyms are never used.
