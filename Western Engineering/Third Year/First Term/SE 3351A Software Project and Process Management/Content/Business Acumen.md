**Business acumen** is the [[PMI Talent Triangle]] leg covering knowledge of and expertise in the industry and organization — what lets a project manager enhance performance and deliver better business outcomes.

Key responsibilities and skills:
- **Strategic alignment** — keeping the project's objectives continuously aligned with the organization's broader business strategy and global trends
- **Value delivery** — understanding the business case, performing cost-benefit analyses (e.g. ROI, NPV), and managing benefits realization
- **Industry expertise** — applying function- or domain-specific knowledge to navigate market positioning, compliance, and competitive analysis
