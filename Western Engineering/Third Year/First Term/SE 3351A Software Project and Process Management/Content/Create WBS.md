**Create WBS** (work breakdown structure) is the last *Scope* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It breaks the deliverables down into **work packages** small enough to manage.

**Output:** the **scope baseline** = project scope statement (from [[Define Scope]]) + WBS + WBS dictionary

> [!example] LMS project
> Decompose the assessment module into work packages for quizzes and rubrics.
