**Develop Schedule** is the last *Schedule* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It pulls together activities ([[Define Activities]]), dependencies ([[Sequence Activities]]), durations ([[Estimate Activity Durations]]) and resource constraints into a timetable that actually works.

**Output:** schedule baseline · project schedule · schedule data

> Set the LMS pilot and launch dates once testing time and the start of term are accounted for.
