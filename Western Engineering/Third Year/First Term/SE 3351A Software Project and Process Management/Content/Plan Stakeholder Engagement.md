**Plan Stakeholder Engagement** is the *Stakeholder* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It picks how to engage each [[Stakeholder|stakeholder]] based on their interests and influence — the follow-on to [[Identify Stakeholders]], and where tools like the [[Stakeholder Grid]] pay off.

**Output:** stakeholder engagement plan

> [!example] LMS project
> Bring accessibility staff in early, and give instructors structured reviews during the pilot.
