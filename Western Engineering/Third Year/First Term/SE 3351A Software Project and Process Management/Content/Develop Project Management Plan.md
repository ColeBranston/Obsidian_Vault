**Develop Project Management Plan** is the *Integration* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It merges every subsidiary plan and baseline into a single, coordinated approach for the whole project.

**Output:** the project management plan, which carries the scope baseline ([[Create WBS]]), schedule baseline ([[Develop Schedule]]) and cost baseline ([[Determine Budget]]).

> [!example] LMS project
> Bring the LMS release scope, budget, schedule and change procedure together into one plan.
