**Identify Stakeholders** is the process of finding who is impacted by a [[Project]], or who could influence it — including decision makers and team members.

Business documents such as the statement of work and the business case are used as inputs, and findings are recorded in a **stakeholder register**.

> [!tip] It never fully stops
> You can only stop identifying stakeholders after the project is closed. Even at deployment/go-live, or when the product moves to ongoing operations, new stakeholders can still surface.
