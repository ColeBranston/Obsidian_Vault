**Three-point estimation** handles uncertainty by taking three effort or cost estimates instead of one, then combining them with the **PERT** (Program Evaluation and Review Technique) weighted average.

| Estimate | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Optimistic (O)** | Everything goes particularly well |
| **Most likely (M)** | Normal working conditions |
| **Pessimistic (P)** | Significant difficulties arise |

$$E = \frac{O + 4M + P}{6}$$

> [!example] One feature
> A feature could take 10, 15 or 26 hours → E = (10 + 4 × 15 + 26) / 6 = 96 / 6 = **16 hours**.

**When to use:** when tasks involve uncertainty, unfamiliar technologies, complex integrations or other risks.
