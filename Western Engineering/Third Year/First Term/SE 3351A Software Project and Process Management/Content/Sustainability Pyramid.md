The **sustainability pyramid** gives the preferred order for managing a project's negative outcomes. Work down the list only when the option above it is genuinely unavailable:

1. **Avoid** — do not cause the harm in the first place
2. **Minimize** — reduce what cannot be avoided
3. **Restore** — repair what was affected
4. **Compensate / Offset** — make good elsewhere, as a last resort

Part of the *integrate sustainability* principle in the [[Project Management Mindset]]. Sustainability here covers **environmental, social and economic** impact, and is a joint accountability across all project areas and phases.
