**Software cost estimation** is the process of predicting the money, effort and time needed to build and maintain a software system. It is the software-specific toolkit behind [[Estimate Costs]].

- The profession has spent decades trying to establish estimation methods that are accurate, reliable and consistent, and still struggles.
- **Why it's hard:** software projects are highly dynamic and don't repeat from one project to the next, which makes estimation one of the most complex parts of project management.

**Common methods:** [[Expert Judgment]] · [[Analogous Estimation]] · [[Bottom-Up Estimation]] · [[Three-Point Estimation]] (PERT) · [[Parametric Estimation]] ([[COCOMO]]) · [[Function Point Estimation]]
