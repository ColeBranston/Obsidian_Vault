**Analogous estimation** starts from the actual effort or cost of a similar, previously completed project, then adjusts it for differences in size, complexity, technology and team experience.

> [!example] Student portal
> A previous student portal cost \$80,000 and the proposed system should need 25% more effort → 80,000 × 1.25 = **\$100,000**.

**When to use:** early in a project, when detailed requirements aren't available yet but historical data is. Usually paired with [[Expert Judgment]].
