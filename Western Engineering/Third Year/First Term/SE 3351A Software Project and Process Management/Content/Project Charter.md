A **project charter** is the document that formally authorizes a [[Project]] to begin. Once the sponsor signs it, the project manager can apply organizational resources to project activities.

It is a high-level document that records:
- **Business justification**: why the project exists
- **Measurable objectives** and their related success criteria
- **Key deliverables, milestones, and roles and responsibilities**, so every [[Stakeholder]] has the same understanding of them

It is the output of [[Develop Project Charter]].
