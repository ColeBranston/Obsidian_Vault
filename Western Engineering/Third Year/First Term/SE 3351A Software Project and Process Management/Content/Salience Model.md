The **salience model** is a stakeholder classification model built on three variables — power, legitimacy, and urgency — shown as a Venn diagram.

- **Power** — the same meaning as in the [[Stakeholder Grid]].
- **Legitimacy** — the stakeholder is appropriately involved in or impacted by the project.
- **Urgency** — how quickly the stakeholder needs immediate attention or regular information about the project.
