**Process groups** (PMI's *focus areas*) represent the chronological steps or phases of a project's life cycle. They define the sequence of actions and oversee the **"how" and "when"** of execution.

1. **Initiating** — authorize the project and identify who has a stake in it
2. **Planning** — consolidate the plans that define scope, schedule, cost and risk
3. **Executing** — guide the team's daily work to produce the deliverables
4. **Monitoring and Controlling** — measure progress and evaluate changes against the plan
5. **Closing** — hand off deliverables and archive lessons learned

Pair with [[Knowledge Areas]], which cover the *what* rather than the *when*. Together they organize the 49 [[Project Management Process|processes]].
