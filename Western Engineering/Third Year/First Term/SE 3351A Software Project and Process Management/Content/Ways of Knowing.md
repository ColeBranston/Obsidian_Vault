**Ways of Knowing** is the [[PMI Talent Triangle]] leg covering the knowledge, skills, and behaviors tied to a project's specific domains — the technical craft of performing the PM role.

Key responsibilities and skills:
- **Selecting and tailoring the delivery approach** — predictive (waterfall), adaptive (agile), or hybrid
- **Managing core constraints** — scope, budget, schedule, resources, quality, risk
- **Applying specific techniques** — brainstorming, change management, producing project artifacts
