A **stakeholder grid** is a classification model a project manager uses to prioritize stakeholders and choose a management approach, based on their power, interest, influence, or impact on the project. There are three types, of which the power/interest grid is the most common:
- Power and interest grid
- Power and influence grid
- Impact and influence grid

**Power** — the stakeholder's level of authority on the project. High power means they can cancel the project or change its direction. Examples: sponsors · customers · key decision makers · PMOs · the project leadership team · stakeholders with sign-off responsibilities · senior (C-level) executives.

**Interest** — the level and frequency of information the stakeholder needs about the project. High interest means they need regular updates; low interest means occasional snippets suffice. Examples: the project team · vendors working on the project · customers · PMOs · key decision makers · the project leadership team · stakeholders with sign-off responsibilities.
