Every project must deliver on five factors that pull against each other:

- **Scope** — the deliverable(s)
- **Time** — the deadline
- **Cost** — the approved budget
- **Quality** — required performance level
- **Expectations** — stakeholder approval

Pulling on one affects the others — e.g., compressing time can pressure cost or quality.
