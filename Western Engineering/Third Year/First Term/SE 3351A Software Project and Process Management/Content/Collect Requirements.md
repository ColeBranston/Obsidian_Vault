**Collect Requirements** is a *Scope* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It draws out and records what [[Stakeholder|stakeholders]] need, along with the acceptance criteria they will judge the result by — the PM-side view of [[Requirements Elicitation]].

**Output:** requirements documentation · requirements traceability matrix

> [!example] LMS project
> Interview students and instructors about accessibility needs and grading workflows.
