**Direction of influence** describes who a project manager is influencing, relative to their own position in the organization. Influencing isn't forcing someone — it's winning people over to do what's best for the project.

- **Upward** — stakeholders hierarchically above you (senior management)
- **Downward** — stakeholders hierarchically below you (the team, junior analysts)
- **Sideward** — peers within the organization, neither above nor below you, but still impacted by or able to influence the project
- **Outward** — stakeholders outside your organization (vendors, suppliers)
