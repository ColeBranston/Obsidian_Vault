The **PMBOK process matrix** lays out all 49 [[Project Management Process|project management processes]] on a grid: [[Knowledge Areas]] down the side (the *what*), [[Process Groups]] across the top (the *when*). Every process lives in exactly one cell.

| Knowledge Area | Initiating (2) | Planning (24) | Executing (10) | Monitoring & Controlling (12) | Closing (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Integration** (7) | [[Develop Project Charter]] | [[Develop Project Management Plan]] | Direct & Manage Project Work<br>Manage Project Knowledge | Monitor & Control Project Work<br>Perform Integrated Change Control | Close Project or Phase |
| **2. Scope** (6) | — | [[Plan Scope Management]]<br>[[Collect Requirements]]<br>[[Define Scope]]<br>[[Create WBS]] | — | Validate Scope<br>Control Scope | — |
| **3. Schedule** (6) | — | [[Plan Schedule Management]]<br>[[Define Activities]]<br>[[Sequence Activities]]<br>[[Estimate Activity Durations]]<br>[[Develop Schedule]] | — | Control Schedule | — |
| **4. Cost** (4) | — | [[Plan Cost Management]]<br>[[Estimate Costs]]<br>[[Determine Budget]] | — | Control Costs | — |
| **5. Quality** (3) | — | [[Plan Quality Management]] | Manage Quality | Control Quality | — |
| **6. Resource** (6) | — | [[Plan Resource Management]]<br>[[Estimate Activity Resources]] | Acquire Resources<br>Develop Team<br>Manage Team | Control Resources | — |
| **7. Communications** (3) | — | [[Plan Communications Management]] | Manage Communications | Monitor Communications | — |
| **8. Risk** (7) | — | [[Plan Risk Management]]<br>[[Identify Risks]]<br>[[Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis]]<br>[[Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis]]<br>[[Plan Risk Responses]] | Implement Risk Responses | Monitor Risks | — |
| **9. Procurement** (3) | — | [[Plan Procurement Management]] | Conduct Procurements | Control Procurements | — |
| **10. Stakeholder** (4) | [[Identify Stakeholders]] | [[Plan Stakeholder Engagement]] | Manage Stakeholder Engagement | Monitor Stakeholder Engagement | — |

> [!tip] Reading the grid
> Planning holds almost half the processes (24/49), and every knowledge area has at least one — see [[Planning Process Group]]. Only Integration reaches all five groups.

> [!note] Which PMBOK edition?
> The 49-process / 5-group structure comes from the **PMBOK® Guide, Sixth Edition** (the slide notes cite it), whereas the [[Project Management Mindset]] comes from the Eighth Edition.
