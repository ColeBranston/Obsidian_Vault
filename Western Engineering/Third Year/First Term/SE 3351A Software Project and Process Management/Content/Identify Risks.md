**Identify Risks** is a *Risk* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It records the specific **threats and opportunities** that could affect the project's objectives.

**Output:** risk register · risk report

> [!example] LMS project
> Log integration delays (a threat) and early pilot recruitment (an opportunity) as risks.

> [!tip] Risk ≠ only bad news
> A risk is any uncertainty that could affect objectives, so positive ones (opportunities) go in the register too.
