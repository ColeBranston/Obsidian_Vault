**Project management** is the application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet project requirements — guiding the work to deliver its intended outcomes.

Teams get there via **predictive**, **hybrid**, or **adaptive** approaches.
*— Project Management Institute (PMI)*

**In practice, it means:**
- Defining a [[Project]], planning, executing, monitoring progress, overcoming obstacles, managing risk, and correcting course
- Balancing desired results (scope, performance, quality) against constraints (time, cost)
- Leading a never-before team through a never-before task, on a fixed budget and timeline
