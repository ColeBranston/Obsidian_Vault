**Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis** is a *Risk* process in the [[Planning Process Group]]. It puts numbers on the project's **combined** risk exposure — used only when the project is complex enough to justify it.

**Output:** updated risk report · updates to related project documents

> [!example] LMS project
> Model the probability of missing the semester launch date.

> [!tip] Qualitative vs quantitative
> [[Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis|Qualitative]] ranks individual risks and is always done; quantitative models their overall numeric effect and is optional.
