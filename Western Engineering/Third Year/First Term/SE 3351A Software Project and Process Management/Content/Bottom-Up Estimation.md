**Bottom-up estimation** splits the project into small, manageable **work packages** (typically via the WBS from [[Create WBS]]), estimates the effort and cost of each one, then adds them up to get the project total.

$$\text{Total Cost} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{Cost of Work Package}_i$$

> [!example] Appointment system
> The team separately estimates authentication, appointment booking, notifications, testing and deployment, then sums them for the total project cost.

**When to use:** during detailed project planning, once the scope and deliverables are understood well enough.
