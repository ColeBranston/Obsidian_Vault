**Power skills** are the [[PMI Talent Triangle]] leg covering the knowledge, skills, and behaviors needed to guide, motivate, and direct a team toward an organization's business goals.

Key responsibilities and skills:
- **Communication** — dominates up to 90% of a project manager's job; vital for setting expectations and aligning stakeholders
- **Interpersonal dynamics** — emotional intelligence, empathy, and conflict resolution to maintain a collaborative team
- **Stakeholder engagement** — building relationships, negotiating, and maintaining influence across a diverse group of stakeholders to drive change
