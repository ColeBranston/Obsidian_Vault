### Major Project Constraints
The deck's version of [[Competing Demands]], expanded slightly:

| Constraint | Notes |
|---|---|
| Scope | The deliverable(s) |
| Schedule | The deadline (= *Time* in the earlier lecture) |
| Budget | The approved cost (= *Cost*) |
| Resources | People, tools, materials available |
| Quality | Required performance level |
| Risk | Uncertainty that could affect any of the above |
| Risk/Expectations | Stakeholder approval (= *Expectations*) |
