**Hypertext** is a collection of documents connected by hyperlinks, so a reader moves between documents by following links rather than reading in a fixed order.

**Hypermedia** generalizes the idea beyond text — images, audio and video become link endpoints too.

The idea long predates the web:

| Year | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1934 | Paul Otlet | *Traité de documentation* |
| 1945 | Vannevar Bush | the hypothetical **Memex** |
| 1968 | Ted Nelson | coined the term *hypertext* |

Tim Berners-Lee's contribution in 1989 was not the concept but a working implementation of it across a network — see [[World Wide Web]].
