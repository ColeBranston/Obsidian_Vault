**Root cause analysis** means tracing a failure back to *why* it happened rather than patching until the symptom disappears.

The premise: a crash or unexpected behaviour is evidence of a gap in your understanding, not just a broken line of code. Learning happens at the moment you make a mistake — but only if you diagnose it. Fix the symptom without understanding it and the gap stays.

Always ask **"Why?"** and then actually find the answer:
- Why this technology? Why this particular way?
- Why isn't it working as intended? Why is it crashing?

> [!tip] This is also how you're examined
> Lecture notes typically describe the *effect* for a given cause. Quiz and exam questions typically go the other way — given an effect, name the cause. Memorizing the notes doesn't build that direction.
