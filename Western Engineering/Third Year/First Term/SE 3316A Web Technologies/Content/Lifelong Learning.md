**Lifelong learning** is a core tenet of a Western education. In engineering terms, its most practical facet is the ability to pick up new technologies on your own — arguably the single most valuable skill an engineer has, since the specific stack you learn in school will be obsolete long before your career is.

Web technologies churn faster than most fields, so this course is deliberately full of opportunities to practise it: much of HTML/CSS is left to self-study, and the labs push you into tools that were never lectured on.

See [[Learning a New Technology]] for a concrete process.
