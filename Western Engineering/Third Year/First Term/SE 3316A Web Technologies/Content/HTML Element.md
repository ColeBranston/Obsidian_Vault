An **element** is the unit of [[HTML]] markup: an opening tag, content, and a closing tag.

```html
<a href="http://www.centralpark.com">Central Park</a>
```

- **Element name** — `a`
- **Attribute** — `href="http://www.centralpark.com"`, a name/value pair on the opening tag
- **Content** — `Central Park`; may be text or other elements nested inside

An **empty element** has no content and closes itself with a trailing slash: `<br />`.

**Global attributes** are the 13 attributes usable on *any* element — `id`, `title`, `lang`, `class`, `style`, `data-*` and friends. `class` and `id` are what [[CSS Selectors]] hook into.
