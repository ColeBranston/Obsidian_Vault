The **World Wide Web** was invented by Tim Berners-Lee at CERN in 1989: [[Hypertext]] carried across the Internet, replacing FTP as the way documents were shared.

Three constituents, each solving a different problem:

| Piece | Job |
|---|---|
| [[HTML]] | an SGML language for expressing hypertext |
| [[URL]] | a notation for locating files on servers |
| **HTTP** | a high-level protocol for transferring those files |

The key to its success was that all three were **vendor neutral** — no single company owned the format, the naming scheme or the protocol, so anyone could implement them.
