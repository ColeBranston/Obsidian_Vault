The **Web Hypertext Application Technology Working Group**, founded by individuals from Apple, Mozilla and Opera, focuses on the HTML5 standard and aims for tighter involvement from the people who actually ship browsers.

Its spec differs slightly from [[W3C]]'s HTML5, and since 2019 WHATWG has taken over the HTML specification outright, maintaining it as a **living standard** — continuously updated rather than frozen and versioned.

The spec lives at `html.spec.whatwg.org`. Worth reading not only for [[HTML Validity]] rules but as a fantastic worked example of [[Structure Versus Presentation]] in its own right.
