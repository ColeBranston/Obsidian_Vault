A four-step approach for absorbing an unfamiliar technology, as practised by the instructor:

1. **Read voraciously for the first few days** — what problem does it solve · what are the alternatives · why this one over an alternative · what are the likely pitfalls
2. **Get your feet wet** with a small tutorial
3. **Apply it narrowly** — pick a small part of your current project and see how the new technology fits
4. **Scale up** to increasingly complex parts

The ordering matters: step 1 is about *judgement* (is this the right tool?), not syntax. Jumping straight to step 2 leaves you fluent in a technology you can't justify using.

Part of [[Lifelong Learning]].
