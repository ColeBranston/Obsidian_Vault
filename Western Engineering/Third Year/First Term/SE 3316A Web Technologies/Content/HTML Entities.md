An **entity** is [[HTML]]'s mechanism for writing characters you otherwise couldn't type literally — either because they're part of HTML syntax (`<`, `>`, `=`, `"`, `&`, `#`) or because they're symbols not on your keyboard.

An entity begins with an ampersand and ends with a semicolon: `&lt;` for `<` · `&gt;` for `>` · `&copy;` for ©.

Any [[Unicode]] character can be written as a **numeric character reference** by its [[Code Point]] — `&#12373;` in decimal, `&#x3055;` in hexadecimal.

Full list: `dev.w3.org/html5/html-author/charref`.
