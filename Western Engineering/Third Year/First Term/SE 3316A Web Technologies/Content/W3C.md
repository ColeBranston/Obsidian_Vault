The **World Wide Web Consortium** develops [[HTML]], [[CSS]] and most other web technology. Founded in 1994, directed by Tim Berners-Lee, with around 380 member companies and organizations, and hosted at MIT (US), Inria (France) and Keio (Japan).

**Players:** members (\$50,000/year) · the Team · an Advisory Board · the Technical Architecture Group · Working Groups.

**Document track**, roughly in order of maturity: Working Draft → Candidate Recommendation → Proposed Recommendation → **Recommendation**. Alongside these sit Working Group Notes, Member Submissions, Staff Comments and Team Submissions.

**Principles:** consensus among members · limited intellectual property rights · free web access to technical reports, unlike ISO.

Its slow, consensus-driven process during the long XHTML years is what pushed browser makers to innovate on their own and eventually to form [[WHATWG]].
