
##### The Recording Process Illustrated

###### Overview of the Recording Process

- Three steps: Analyze, Journalize, and Post.
    
- Focus on October 2021 transactions for Sierra Corporation.
    
- Step 1: Determine if an accounting transaction has occurred.
    
- Step 2: Prepare the journal entry.
    
- Step 3: Post the journal entry to the general ledger.
    

###### Transaction Details

- Transaction Date: October 1, 2021.
    
- Cash investment: $10,000 in exchange for 10,000 common shares.
    

###### Steps in the Recording Process

- **Basic Analysis:**
    
    - Asset Cash increased by $10,000.
        
    - Shareholders' equity account Common Shares increased by $10,000.
        
- **Equation Analysis:**
    
    - Accounting equation: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders’ Equity.
        
    - Assets: Cash +$10,000; Shareholders' Equity: Common Shares +$10,000.
        
    - No liabilities recorded.
        
- **Debit-Credit Analysis:**
    
    - Debit Cash: $10,000 (increases assets).
        
    - Credit Common Shares: $10,000 (increases equity).
        
- **Journal Entry:**
    
    - Date: October 1.
        
    - Debit: Cash $10,000.
        
    - Credit: Common Shares $10,000.
        
    - Description: Issued common shares.
        
- **Posting:**
    
    - Cash T-account: Beginning balance of $10,000 on the debit side.
        
    - Common Shares T-account: Posting of $10,000 on the credit side.

###### Trial Balance Preparation

- A trial balance is a list of general ledger accounts and their balances at a specific time.
    
- It is prepared at the end of an accounting period (monthly, quarterly, or annually).
    
- Main purpose: to ensure that debits equal credits after posting.
    
- Useful in the preparation of financial statements.
    

##### Procedure for Preparing a Trial Balance

###### Outline of the Procedure

- List account titles and balances in order from the general ledger and chart of accounts.
    
- Enter debit balances in the left-hand column and credit balances in the right-hand column.
    
- Total both columns and ensure they are equal.
    

###### Steps to Prepare a Trial Balance

- Organize account titles and balances as per financial statement order.
    
- Record debit balances on the left and credit balances on the right.
    
- Calculate totals for both debit and credit columns.
    
- Verify that total debits equal total credits (e.g., $37,700 = $37,700).
