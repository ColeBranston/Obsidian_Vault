
**No one framework exists to evaluate all comprehensive cases; however,**  
**these STEPS are commonly used:**  

1. Understand the role, goals and constraints of the decision-maker.  
2. Clearly outline all necessary decision and recommendations to be made.  
3. Determine the type of analysis required to make each decision/recommendation.  
4. Understand the organization.  
5. Assess opportunities, perform analysis to make decisions.  
6. Evaluate overall strategy effectiveness.  
#### Understanding the Roles, Goals, & Constraints

**Outline qualitative and quantitative objectives.**  
- These criteria should be used to evaluate alternatives and form decisions.  

**Document any constraints and discuss how the potential obstacles** **can be managed.**  
- These constraints should be factored into decisions and recommendations; otherwise, recommendations may not be implementable.
#### Outline All Necessary Decisions & Recommendations

**Read the case thoroughly for any “analysis triggers”.**  
- Look for the words “wondered”, “wanted to”, etc. to identify additional decisions and recommendations to be made.  

**Understand the interconnectedness of decisions and recommendations.**

	• What decisions depend on others?  
	• What decisions can be made independently of others?  
	• Do any decisions require follow up recommendations?  

>[!example] Example 1
>If you decide to launch the new product-line, how will you recommend the $10,000 advertising budget be spent?  

>[!example] Example 2
>If you decide to purchase the new equipment, where will you recommend the business obtain financing?

#### Understand the Organization

**A look at the business itself and the environment the firm is working within:**

1. Business Size-Up
	• Types of analysis may include: industry/market, PEST, consumer, competitive and corporate capabilities  
	• What factors are working for the company?  
	• What factors are working against the company?

2. Financial Size-Up
	• Understand the past financial performance of the business  
	• Types of analysis may include: statement of cash flows preparation, and interpretation, ratio preparation and interpretation, contribution and sub-unit  
	• What does this mean for future opportunities? Should changes be made?

#### Assess Opportunities & Make Decisions

• What are the **qualitative** considerations?  
• What are the **quantitative** results?  
• How does **sensitivity analysis** affect the results?  
• How do the **goals and constraints** influence your decisions?

#### Strategy Effectiveness

- **Projected statements** will demonstrate the effectiveness of the decisions.
- What do the results of the projected statements reveal?  
	- Projected statement of earnings will reveal changes in profitability.  
	- Projected statement of financial position will expose financing requirements.

#### What Constitutes a Good Comprehensive Case Analysis?

Answers to these questions will reveal if a good case analysis has been  
performed.

- Has the appropriate analysis been performed? 
	- Have implications (conclusions) been drawn from the case facts?  
	- Do the implications relate to the decisions and recommendations to be made?

- Have all decisions and recommendations been made?  
- Are the decisions consistent with the analysis?  
- Are the decisions well supported?
- Are the proposed changes implementable? 
- Have the results of the decisions on the company as a whole been analyzed?
