
- Accounting for discounts, returns and allowances can be very complicated because the entry required depends on your role in the situation. It is very important that you understand what company you are recording transactions for so that you know if your company is the **buyer** or the **seller**.

- Most companies are both the **buyer** and the seller **at** different points. You must isolate the specific event in question to determine if the company is the **buyer** or the **seller** for that specific situation.

- If your company is purchasing inventory, then it is the role of the **buyer** that you should be concerned about. If your company is selling goods, then it is the role of the **seller** that you should be focused on.

- If you determine that your company is the **SELLER**, the terms associated with the entries are:

	- Sales revenue
	- Sales discounts
	- Sales returns & allowances

___
## Entry

The entry to record total sales (i.e., cash and credit sales) is:

	DR Cash  
	DR Accounts receivable (A/R)  
		CR Sales revenue

And there is a need to create two contra-revenue accounts, Sales Discounts and Sales Returns and Allowances.

Discount terms are often given in the following format—3/10, net 30 where:

- A 3 per cent discount is granted if payment is received during the discount period.

- The discount period is 10 days.

- The account is due in 30 days.

**Note:** The discount period can start on the date the goods are shipped or the date that goods are received, depending on negotiations between the supplier and the customer. For purposes of this course, you will always be told when the discount period starts.

When the balance is collected in time to receive the discount, the entry to record this collection is:

	DR Cash  
	DR Sales discounts  
		CR Accounts receivable (A/R)

- A sales allowance occurs when the seller decides to grant a deduction from the selling price. This may occur if the customer is dissatisfied with the merchandise or if the seller is trying to turn over inventory, for example:

The entry to record a sales returns or allowance:

	DR Sales returns & allowances  
		CR Accounts receivable (A/R) or Cash
