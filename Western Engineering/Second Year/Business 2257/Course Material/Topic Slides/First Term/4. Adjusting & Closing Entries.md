#### Adjusting Entries

>[!PURPOSE]
>- To update the records
>	- Ensure that **revenues** are recorded *in the period* **earned**
>	- Ensure that **expenses** are recorded *in the period* **incurred**

- Prepared at the **end** of the period
	- It would be too time consuming to record adjustments every day.

>[!Adjusting Entries Summary]
>![[Pasted image 20240911002503.png]]

##### Deferred Expense Example...

**EXAMPLE: Frisbees United**
*At the beginning of fiscal 2017, the company paid $6,000 for a 2-year insurance policy and $2,500 for supplies*

- Originally, the purchases would be recorded as:

___

**DR** Prepaid Insurance $\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;$ $6,000
	**CR** Cash $\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;$$6,000

**DR** Supplies $\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;$ $2,500
	**CR** Cash $\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;$$2,500
	
___

This correctly records both of these payments as assets.

At the end of the fiscal year, one year of the two-year insurance policy has expired, or been "used up".

- Therefore, the adjusting entry would be:

	**DR** Insurance Expense: $3,000
		**CR** Prepaid Insurance: $3,000

- In addition, $1,500 worth of supplies were still on hand

	**DR** Supplies Expense: $1,000 $*$
		**CR** Supplies: $\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;\;$ $1,000

**O/B($0) + Purchases ($2,500) - Remaining ($1,500) = $1,000**

#### Closing Entries

>[!PURPOSE]
>- To **transfer** net income/loss to **Retained Earnings**
>- To establish a **zero balance** in each of the temporary accounts

##### Permanent vs. Temporary Accounts

>[!Permanent Accounts]
>**Statement of Financial Position** accounts whose balances are carried into the next accounting period (assets, liabilities, & equity)

>[!Temporary Accounts]
>**Income Statement** accounts that are closed to the Retained Earnings account at the end of the accounting period (revenues & expenses)
>>[!Income Summary]
>>- A special temporary account used to close the revenue and expense accounts.

#### Closing Entry - Steps

1. 
	a. **Debit** revenue accounts will credit balances, **Credit** Income Summary
	b. **Credit** revenue accounts with debit balances, **Debit** Income Summary

2. 
	a. **Credit** expense accounts with debit balances, **Debit** Income Summary
	b. **Debit** expense accounts with credit balances, **Credit** Income Summary

3. *Subtotal* Income Summary, calculate and record **taxes**. **Debit** Income Tax Expense, **Credit** income Tax Payable.

4. **Credit** Income Tax Expense, **Debit** Income Summary

5. **Close** Income Summary to Retained Earnings

**Note:** If there is a net loss, skip steps **3** and **4**
