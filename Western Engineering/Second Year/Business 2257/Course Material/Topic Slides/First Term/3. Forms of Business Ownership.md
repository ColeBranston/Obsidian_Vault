## Advantages & Disadvantages (SOLE PROP/PAR, CORP.)

### Corporations

___
**Advantages:** 
- have limited liability (shareholders not being personally liable for corporate debts)
- ownership easily transferred
- more favorable tax treatment (depends)

**Disadvantages:**

- increased government regulations
- taxed as a separate legal entity
___

### Partnerships & Proprietorships

___

**Advantages**:
- easier to form
- not taxed as separate entities
- owners (partners and proprietors) pay personal income tax on their share of profit
	→ May be an advantage or disadvantage

**Disadvantages**:

- owners are personally liable
- difficulty in obtaining financing 

___

## Corporations (Public VS. Private)

- Can only be two types: **Public**, **Private**
### Public

___
- shares are traded public on stock exchanges. Ex. TSX, Nasdaq
- anyone can purchase shares
- large number of shareholders, often thousands or even hundred of thousands
___
### Private

___

- shares are not traded publicly
- traded shares are handled privately through brokerage firms
- have far fewer shareholders (usually): one to a few shareholders

___
