- Whenever possible, **perform the entire calculation** in your calculator to  
avoid rounding errors. Ex. **Any Calculation**

### T-Accounts
___
When entering numbers into **T-ACCOUNTS**, round to the nearest  
dollar **($)** 

- The one exception to this rule is with **bank reconciliation exercises** where you  
	should include decimal places recording the precise amount on both the bank  
	reconciliation and in the adjusting entries
___
### Revenue & Expense
___
When determining **REVENUE** (e.g., interest revenue) or **EXPENSE** (e.g.,  
interest expense, depreciation expense) calculations, round to the nearest  
half month **(1/2 month)**
___

### Other (Inventory, Bonds, Allocation Rates)

___

**INVENTORY**: When calculating unit cost per order, round unit costs to  
three decimal places **(.xxx)**

**BONDS**: Coupon interest payments must be rounded to the nearest  
dollar **($)**

**ALLOCATION RATES:** Round to the nearest percentage **(%)**
___
