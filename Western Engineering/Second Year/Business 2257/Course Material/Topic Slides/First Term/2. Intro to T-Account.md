## To make an entry in a T-account:

___

1. Put the dollar amount on the appropriate side (debit or credit) 
	- No need to use the dollar sign.

2. It is very important that you use trail numbers to keep track of the entry.

## Types of Accounts

___

- There are five basic types of accounts:

**STATEMENT OF FINANCIAL POSITION** (Accounts)

- Assets
- Liabilities
- Equity

**INCOME STATEMENT** (Accounts)

- Revenue
- Expenses
___
## Accounts (Cont'd)
### Assets

___

- Economic resources (i.e. things of value) owned by a business that are expected to benefit future operations. Ex. Car, Computer, and Building

___

### Liabilities
___

- Debts or obligations of a business. Ex. Accounts Payable (A/P), and Mortgage Payable

___
### Equity

___

- The excess of assets over liabilities.

___

### Revenue

___

- The price of goods sold or services rendered during a given accounting  
period. Ex. Sales, Legal Fees Earned and Interest Revenue

___
### Expenses

___

- The cost of all goods and services used up or expended in the process of  
earning revenue during an accounting period. Ex. Rent Expense, Advertising Expense and Insurance Expense

___
## Fundamental Accounting Equation

___

***Very Important***

$$Equity = Assets \; - \; Liabilties$$

**Additionally...**

$$Equity=Shares \; + \; Retained\;Earnings$$
$$Retained\;Earnings=Revenues \; - \; Expenses \; -\; Dividends$$
