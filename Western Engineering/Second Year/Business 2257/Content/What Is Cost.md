**Cost:** 

1. Goods or services used to generate revenue
2. A recurring flow

**Investment:**

1. Benefits many periods  (only a portion of amount is cost)
2. A one-time flow

### Cost Behavior 

**Fixed Costs:**

- Do no change with changes in activity level
- eg. rent, supervisor salary, interest on loan

![[Pasted image 20250107044428.png]]

**Variable Costs:**

- Change directly with variable in activity level  
- e.g., raw materials, direct labor

![[Pasted image 20250107044538.png]]

**Semi-Variable Costs**

- A portion of the cost changes directly with variables in activity level
- Combined fixed and variable cost  
- e.g., utilities

![[Pasted image 20250107044658.png]]

**Step Costs:**

- Do no change in direct proportion with changes in volume, but do change in large amounts at distinct volumes  
- Can be viewed as fixed costs within a narrow relevant range  
- e.g., production supervisor if a second shift is added
