### Calculating

We calculate the expense related to uncollectable accounts for the  
period using the aging of accounts receivable method/percentage  
of accounts receivable method.

##### Using the Ageing of Accounts Receivable Method

The aging of accounts receivable method **estimates how many of the**  
**outstanding balance in accounts receivable at the end of the**  
**period** will be uncollectable.

The company’s management determines, on average, what proportion,  
or percentage, of outstanding accounts receivable are typically  
uncollectable. This value then becomes the ending balance in  
allowance for doubtful accounts.

>[!Equation] 
>Ending Balance A4DA = Ending Balance A/R x Estimated %

*Summary*

Step 1. Calculate Trial Balance in A4DA

Step 2. Calculate Ending Balance in A4DA

- **Ending Balance A4DA** = Ending Balance A/R x Estimated %

Step 3: Calculate Bad Debt Expense

- **Bad Debt Expense = E/B A4DA – T/B A4DA**

Step 4: Record

	DR Bad Debt Expense
		CR A4DA
