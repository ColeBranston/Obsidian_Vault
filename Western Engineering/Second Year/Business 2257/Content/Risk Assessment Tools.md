1. **Margin of Safety**

By how much can projections be off before the venture becomes a breakeven proposition?

$$\text{Margin of Safety} = {\text{Projected Sales - B/E Sales}\over \text{Projected Sales}}$$
2. **Breakeven as a Percentage of Market**

How much of the current market must be captured in order to breakeven?

$$\text{B/E \% of Market} = {\text{B/E Sales (or Units)} \over \text{Total Market in \$ (or units)}}$$
**Interpretation dependent on:**

	a) Definition of the market
	b) Market Characteristics

3. **Return on Investment and Payback**

$$ROI = {\text{Recurring Annual Return} \over \text{Initial Investment}}$$
Measured as a percentage and must be compared to a hurdle rate that is specific to the company, the industry and the venture. The more risky the venture, the higher the hurdle rate. The higher the ROI, the more enticing the venture.

$$\text{Payback} = {\text{Initial Investment}\over \text{Recurring Annual Return}}$$

Measured as the # of years needs for the return to pay back the investment. Alternate expression of ROI (eg. a 2-year payback = a 50% ROI).
