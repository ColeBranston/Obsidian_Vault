The point at which the business earns neither a loss nor a profit (ie. Revenues equal Expenses)

$$\text{B/E Units} = {\text{Fixed Costs} \over \text{Unit Contribution}}$$
$$\text{B/E Units} = {\text{Fixed Costs} \over \text{Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit}}$$
$$\text{B/E Sales} = {\text{Fixed Costs} \over \text{Contribution Margin Ratio}}$$
$$\text{B/E Sales} = {\text{Fixed Costs} \over \text{(SP - VC \ Per Unit)/SP}}$$
**Example:**

Shoetree inc. has the following cost schedule

	• Sales Commissions: $0.60 per pair  
	• Direct Labor: $1.20 per pair  
	• Raw Materials: $0.60 per pair  
	• Factory & Machinery Rent: $40,000/year  
	• Corporate Salaries: $60,000/year  
	• They sell the trees at $4 per pair

$$\text{Unit Contribution} = $4.00 (\text{Selling Price}) - $2.40 (\text{Variable Cost})$$
$$ = $1.60$$
$$\text{Contribution Margin Ratio} = {$4.00 - $2.40 \over$4.00}$$
$$= 40\%$$
$$\text{B/E Units} = {$100,000 (\text{Fixed Costs}) \over $1.60 (\text{Unit Contribution})}$$
$$= 62,500\text{ pairs of shoe trees}$$

$$\text{B/E Sales} = {$100,000 (\text{FC}) \over40\% (\text{CMR})}$$
$$=250,000 \text{ in sales}$$
