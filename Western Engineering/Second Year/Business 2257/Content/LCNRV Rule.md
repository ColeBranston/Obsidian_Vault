>[!LCNRV Rule]
>**Net Realizable Value:** Value the inventory at the estimated selling price less any variable selling costs (e.g., sales commissions).

**The Rule:** Inventory should not be carried at a value **greater** than the  
amount expected to be realized from its sale. When the net realizable  
value of inventory is lower than its cost, inventory is written down to its net  
realizable value.

- LCNRV is applied to the items in inventory after one of the inventory valuation methods: (1) specific identification, (2) FIFO or (3) average cost has been used to determine cost.

## LCNRV Steps

1. Determine the cost of the inventory.
2. Determine the net realizable value of the inventory.
3. Compare the cost valuation (step 1) to the net realizable value (step 2).
4. Record the final ending balance in inventory at the lower of the cost or net realizable value. If the NRV is lower than the cost of the inventory, the difference will be debited to the Cost of Goods Sold Expense and credited to Inventory.
