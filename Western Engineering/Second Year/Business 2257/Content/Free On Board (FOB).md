**FOB SHIPPING POINT:**

- **Supplier** is responsible **up until the point of shipping**  
- **Buyer** is responsible for goods in transit: **Buyer** pays freight

**FOB DESTINATION:**

- **Supplier** is responsible **up until they reach their destination**  
- **Supplier** is responsible for goods in transit: **Supplier** pays freight
