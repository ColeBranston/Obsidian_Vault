
- Specific identification is a good inventory valuation method to use if the company carries high-price, low-volume inventory (e.g., a jewelry store).

- Purchase inventory records must be used to assign actual cost to units in the ending balance. In order to use this inventory valuation method, you must be given the purchase number breakdown for the remaining units.

- **Note**: Unlike other valuation methods, specific identification must be used to cost inventories that are not ordinarily interchangeable. This is applicable for both IFRS and ASPE.
