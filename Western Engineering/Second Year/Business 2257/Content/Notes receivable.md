### Accounting for Notes Receivable

When dealing with a notes receivable, there are three types of entries we  
could be concerned with:

- Creation of the Notes receivable

- Accrual of interest revenue at fiscal year end

- Maturity of the Notes receivable

### Creation of Notes Receivable

When a notes receivable is created, to record this transaction we will  
simply

	DR Notes Receivable  
		CR Cash OR Accounts Receivable

### Notes Receivable Maturity Date

On a notes receivable’s maturity date the note ceases to exist and we  
must account for the following:

1. Interest earned up to the maturity date

2. Collection of the notes receivable + all earned interest

<center> <strong>- OR -</strong></center>

2. Note is **not** collected and notes receivable + all earned interest becomes a current outstanding account receivable

___
