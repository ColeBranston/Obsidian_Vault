### Bad Argument or Argument With Implicit Premise?

- It can be unclear whether an argument includes an implicit premise and, if so, what the premise is.
- Use your best judgement.

### Implicit Premises in Online Exercises

- Sometimes you will be asked to put an argument in standard form and add any implicit premises it might have. When asked to do this, you will be able to choose "implicit" as the premise number to insert an implicit premise, as shown below.

- After selecting “Implicit”, the premise will automatically be assigned a label (starting from “M1”) and you will be asked to select what the implicit premise is from some options.
