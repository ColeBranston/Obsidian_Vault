
>[!important]  Validity and Logical Possibility
>![[Validity]]
>- This means that for an argument to be valid is has to be contradictory for its premises to be true and its conclusion false.
>- Eg.
>(P1) Either Shadi is at the office or at home.
>(P2) Shadi is not at the office.
>(C) Therefore, Shadi is at home.
