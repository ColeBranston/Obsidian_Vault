
Critical thinking is a central educational aim, but other complementary thinking skills, such as **creativity** and **metacognition**, are equally important.

---

#### **Creativity**

- Essential for generating solutions to problems in personal and professional life.
- Works hand-in-hand with critical thinking:
    - **Creativity**: Generates ideas and solutions.
    - **Critical Thinking**: Evaluates and refines them.

---

#### **Metacognition (Thinking About Thinking)**

- Involves understanding, monitoring, and controlling one's own thinking processes.
- Key to **critical thinking**:
    - Becoming aware of one’s reasoning.
    - Identifying strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
- Insights into **human psychology** (e.g., cognitive biases) enhance metacognitive abilities.

---

#### **Qualities for Effective Thinking**

- **Knowledge** of logic and reasoning principles.
- **Self-awareness** of cognitive processes.
- **Personal traits**:
    - Persistence in problem-solving.
    - Desire for self-improvement.

---

#### **Emphasis on Metacognitive Thinking**

- A reflective and disciplined mindset is crucial for improving thinking.
- Metacognition is often underemphasized in critical thinking education.
- Critical thinking should be viewed as part of a broader effort to enhance **metacognitive competence**, which includes:
    1. **Meta-conceptions**: Awareness of one’s own thought processes.
    2. **Knowledge**: General understanding of cognitive processes.
    3. **Self-knowledge**: Insights into personal thinking patterns.
    4. **Self-regulation**: Ability to monitor and adapt one’s thinking.

---

#### **Research Insight**

- Joe Lau (2015) argues for expanding critical thinking education to focus on **metacognition**, particularly at the university level.
- Metacognitive competence helps individuals adapt to rapid changes and improves thinking and learning.

This integrated approach highlights the importance of combining critical thinking with metacognitive skills to foster effective and adaptable thinkers.
