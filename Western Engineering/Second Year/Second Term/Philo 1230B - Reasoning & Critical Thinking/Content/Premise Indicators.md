- Expressions such as the following sometimes suggest that a premise follows:
	- because, since, for, after all,
	- first, second, etc.,
	- firstly, secondly, etc.
	- on the assumption that, assuming that
	- in view of the fact that
	- this follows from
	- this is show/indicates/suggested/supported by
	- this can/may be inferred/deduced/derived from

**Note:** words that sometimes function as premise indicators can occur in the absence of a premise. 

Example: 
- I've been here *since* noon
- I'm *assuming that* you don't want to go to lunch.
