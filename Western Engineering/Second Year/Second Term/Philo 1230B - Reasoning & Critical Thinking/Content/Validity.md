An argument is **valid** if and only if there is no logically possible situation where all the premises are true and the conclusion is false at the same time. (Lau, A03)

ie. When an argument is valid, the truth of the premises guarantees the truth of the conclusion. 

Example:

**(P1)** If horses are mammals, then horses are warm-blooded. 
**(P2)** Horses are mammals. 
**(C)** Therefore, horses are warm-blooded.

- When an argument is not valid, it is **invalid**
