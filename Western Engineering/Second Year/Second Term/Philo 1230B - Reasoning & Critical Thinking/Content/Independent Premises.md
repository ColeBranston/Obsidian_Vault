Two or more premises are independent premises when they work independently to support a conclusion.

**Example**: 
(P1) My neighbor says she has a car. 
(P2) I’ve seen a car in my neighbor’s garage. 
(C) My neighbor has a car. 

- **(P1) and (P2) are independent premises.**

We show that premises are independent of one another by linking them to the conclusion with separate arrows, forming a “V” shape
