
- **Aim**: to show that some statement **S** is true
- This method of arguing proceeds in three steps:

1. Assume that not-**S** true.
2. Show that not-**S** leads to a contradiction or other absurd (clearly false) claim.
3. Conclude that **S** is true

**Examples:**

- Aim: Show that the claim that Flakey Chips should not be considered a food is true.

**Step 1:** Assume that Flakey Chips *should* be considered a food.
**Step 2:** Show that the assumption leads to an absurdity.

	(P1) Flakey Chips should be considered a food. 
	(P2) If Flakey Chips should be considered a food, then wood chips, cardboard, and styrofoam should be considered foods too. 
	(C) Wood chips, cardboard, and styrofoam should be considered foods.

**Step 3:** Conclude that the assumption is false.
