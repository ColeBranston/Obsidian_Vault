- Two or more are co-premises when they work together to support a conclusion. 

**Example**: 
(P1) All horses are mammals. 
(P2) All mammals are warm-blooded. 
(C) Therefore, all horses are warm-blooded. 

- **(P1) and (P2) are co-premises.**

In argument maps, we depict co-premises by linking the arrows joining them to the conclusion using a “Y” shape.
