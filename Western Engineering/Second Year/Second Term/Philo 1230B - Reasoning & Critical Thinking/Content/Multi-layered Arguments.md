***Definition***: A multi-layered argument is an argument in which some premises are supposed to be supported by other premises

![[Pasted image 20250216225031.png]]

- Multi-layered arguments have “multiple layers” of argumentation. 
- Premises that are supposed to be supported by other premises are intermediate conclusions in a multi layered argument.
- The conclusion of a multi-layered argument is the main conclusion.
