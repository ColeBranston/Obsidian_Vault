- X is a **sufficient condition** for Y just in case X guarantees Y.

(equivalently)

- X is a **sufficient condition** for Y just in case it is impossible for X to obtain but not Y.

**Examples:**

- Reding the required readings is all it takes to count as having done the readings.
- Having a valid driver's license is all it takes to be legally allowed to drive in Canada.
- If something is a square, it must be a four-sided figure.
