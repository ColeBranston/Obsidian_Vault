## Necessary and sufficient conditions and possibility and necessity

- Corresponding to the different notions of possibilities and necessity, there are different notions of necessary and sufficient conditions. 

>[!example] Examples
>- having a negative charge is **nomologically necessary** for being an electron.
>- Being a triangle is **logically sufficient** for having three sides.
>- Hvaing a sibling is logically necessary and **logically sufficient** for being a sibling
