
>[!important] Kinds of Possibility
>- There are different kinds of possibility and impossibility
>- **Examples:**
>	- It is impossible for a triangle to have four sides. (**Logically Impossibility**)
>	- It is not possible to travel faster than light. (**Nomological Impossibility**)
>	- You can't drink if you're under 19. (**Legal Impossibility**)
>	- You are permitted to lie to save your life. (**Moral Possibility**)

>[!important] Logical Possibility
>A state of affairs is **logically possible** just in case it is not contradictory
>- A state of affairs is **logically impossible** just incase it is not logically possible (ie. it is contradictory)
>- Example: Traveling faster than the speed of light is logically possible, since it does not contradict the laws of logic. 
>- **Some Examples**
>>[!example] examples
>>- it is **logically impossible** for a triangle to have four sides
>>- It is **logically impossible** for 2 to be odd.
>>- Turning water into gold is **logically possible**
>>- It is **logically possible** for the world to end now.
>>

>[!important] Nomological Possibility
>A state of affairs is **nomologically possible** just incase it does not contradict the laws of nature
>- The laws of nature include the true laws discovered by physics, biology, and other sciences.
>- A state of affairs is **nomologically impossible** just in case it is not nomologically possible
>>[!example] examples
>>- It is **nomologically impossible** to ravel faster than the speed of light.
>>- A two-year course on critical thinking is **nomologically possible**
>>- It is **nomologically possible** for life to exist on other planets.
>>- it is **nomologically Impossible** for humans to survive without oxygen.

### Other Notions of Possibility

Recall that:
- Something is **logically possible** just incase it doesn't contradict the laws of logic.
- Something is **nomologically possible** just in case it doesn't contradict the laws of nature.

Similarly:
- Something is **legally possible** just in case it is permitted by the legal laws.
- Something is **morally possible** just in case it doesn't contradict the rules of morality.

### Other Notions of Possibility Used in Philosophy

- Something is **conceptually possible** just in case it isn't contradictory given our concepts.
- Something is **metaphysically possible** just in case it doesn't contradict the laws of metaphysics.

>[!important] Necessity
>A state of affairs is necessary just incase the state of affairs not obtaining is impossible.
>- A state of affairs is **logically necessary** just in case the state of affairs not obtaining is logically impossible.
>- A state of affairs is **nomologically necessary** just in case the state of affairs not obtaining is nomologically impossible. 
>>[!example] examples
>>- It is logically necessary that 2 is even
>>- It is nomologically necessary that electrons have a negative charge.
>>- It is not nomologically necessary that cats have fur.
