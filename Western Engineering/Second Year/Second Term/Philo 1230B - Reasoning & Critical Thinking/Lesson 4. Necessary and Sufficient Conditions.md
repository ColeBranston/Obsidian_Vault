You need to show your ticket in order to enter the concert hall.

- This statement asserts a relationship between these two conditions:
	1. You show your ticket
	2. You enter the concert hall.

- Condition (1) is required to obtain in order for condition (2) to obtain.

### Necessary and Sufficient Conditions

- The notions of **necessary** and **sufficient conditions** provide us with a clear way of thinking about such relations.

>[!Necessary Conditions]
![[Necessary Conditions]]

### Counter Examples to Claims about Necessary Conditions

A counter example to the claim that X is a necessary condition for Y is a case in which Y obtains but not X.
- eg.
	- Claim: Completing all the exercises is necessary in order to pass this course.
	- Counter example: Becca missed an exercise and got a B.

##### Example 1:

- Is the following claims true or false?

**Having four sides is necessary for being a square.** (True)

- Recall: X is a **necessary condition** for Y just in case X is required for Y.
- Ask: Is having four sides required in order for something to be a square?

##### Example 2:

**Being navy is a necessary condition for being blue.** (False)

- Recall: X is a **necessary condition** for Y just incase it is impossible for Y to obtain without X obtaining.
- Ask: Is it impossible for something to be blue without being navy?

### Counter Examples to Claims About Sufficient Conditions

- To show that X is not a sufficient condition for Y, find a case where X obtains but not Y.

Example:
- Having a valid driver's license is a sufficient condition for being legally allowed to drive in Canada.
- A counter example: the case of someone with a valid drivers license who is above the legal blood alcohol limit. 

##### Example 3

- Is the following claim true or false?

**Love is all you need for happiness** (False)

- In other words: Love is sufficient
- Recall: X is a **sufficient condition** for Y just in case X guarantees Y.
- Ask: Does having love guarantee being love. -> No
