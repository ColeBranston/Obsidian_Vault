
Define a differential vector operator

$$\begin{equation}
\nabla \triangleq \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z}
\end{equation}
$$
Called the **gradient operator** -> Symbol itself is called "Nabla"
	-> Will be referred to by "Grad"

- Only has meaning when applied to a function, used in different kind of ways, first method will be for the grad operator to "operate on" a scalar function.

We would say the following: "the gradient of $F(x,y,z)$ is":

$$\begin{equation}
\nabla F(x, y, z) = \frac{\partial F}{\partial x} \hat{i} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial y} \hat{j} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial z} \hat{k}
\end{equation}
$$
**Notice:** We got a scalar function and in response, got a vector function, each component of which is the partial derivative of the original scalar function with respect to the corresponding variable.  

### Special Notation

If we want to specifically refer to the $x$ component of a gradient (before actually calculating it), we might write: 

$$\begin{equation}
(\nabla F)_x = \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}
\end{equation}
$$
-> This equation would indicate the rate with which $F$ varies along $x$.

**Example:** Calculate the gradient of $G(x,y,z) = xyz$

**Answer:**

$$\begin{align}
\nabla G &= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) G(x, y, z) \\
&= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) xyz \\
&= \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (xyz) + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (xyz) + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} (xyz) \\
&= yz \hat{i} + xz \hat{j} + xy \hat{k}
\end{align}
$$

**You would say that the vector function in the end is the gradient of the initial scalar function $xyz$**.

### Alternative Examples

**Example Problems:** 

$$\begin{align}
Q1: & \quad F(x, y, z) = xyz \\
Q2: & \quad F(x, y) = x^3 y^2 \\
Q3: & \quad H(x, y, z) = x^2 e^y \cos 4z \\
Q4: & \quad G(x, y, z) = \cosh x \\
Q5: & \quad v(x, z) = \frac{\ln(\alpha z^2)}{x} \\
Q6: & \quad d(z, y, x) = 15x + \beta - \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}}
\end{align}
$$

**Q1:**

$$\begin{align}
\nabla F &= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) F(x, y, z) \\
&= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) xyz \\
&= \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (xyz) + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (xyz) + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} (xyz) \\
&= \hat{i} (yz) + \hat{j} (xz) + \hat{k} (xy)
\end{align}
$$
**Q2:**

$$\begin{align}
F(x, y) &= x^3 y^2 \\
\nabla F &= \left[ \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \right] x^3 y^2 \\
&= \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (x^3 y^2) + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (x^3 y^2) \\
&= 3x^2 y^2 \hat{i} + 2x^3 y \hat{j}
\end{align}
$$
**Q3:**

$$\begin{align}
\nabla H &= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) H(x, y, z) \\
&= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) x^2 e^y \cos(4z) \\
&= \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (x^2 e^y \cos(4z)) + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (x^2 e^y \cos(4z)) + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} (x^2 e^y \cos(4z)) \\
&= e^y \cos(4z) \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (x^2) \hat{i} + x^2 \cos(4z) \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (e^y) \hat{j} + x^2 e^y \frac{\partial}{\partial z} (\cos(4z)) \hat{k} \\
&= e^y \cos(4z) (2x) \hat{i} + x^2 \cos(4z) (e^y) \hat{j} + x^2 e^y (-4 \sin(4z)) \hat{k} \\
&= 2x e^y \cos(4z) \hat{i} + x^2 e^y \cos(4z) \hat{j} - 4x^2 e^y \sin(4z) \hat{k}
\end{align}
$$

**Q4:**

$$\begin{align}
G(x, y, z) &= \cosh(z) \\
\nabla G &= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) G(x, y, z) \\
&= \left( \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \cosh(z) \\
&= \hat{i} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (\cosh(z)) + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} (\cosh(z)) + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} (\cosh(z)) \\
&= 0 \hat{i} + 0 \hat{j} + \sinh(z) \hat{k} \\
&= \sinh(z) \hat{k}
\end{align}
$$

**Q5:**

$$\begin{align}
v(x, z) &= \frac{\ln(\alpha z^2)}{x} \\
\nabla v &= \left( \hat{\imath} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \frac{\ln(\alpha z^2)}{x} \\
&= \ln(\alpha z^2) \left( -\frac{1}{x^2} \right) \hat{\imath} + \frac{1}{x} \left( \frac{1}{\alpha z^2} \right) (2 \alpha z) \hat{k} \\
&= -\frac{\ln(\alpha z^2)}{x^2} \hat{\imath} + \left( \frac{2}{xz} \right) \hat{k}
\end{align}
$$
**Q6:**

$$\begin{align}
\nabla d &= \left[ \hat{\imath} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} + \hat{j} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} + \hat{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right] \left( 15x + \beta - \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}} \right) \\
&= 15 \hat{\imath} - \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \left( \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}} \right) \hat{j} - \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \left( \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}} \right) \hat{k} \\
&= 15 \hat{\imath} - \left( \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{z^{1/2} (-3/2)}{y^{5/2}} \right) \hat{j} - \left( \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^3}} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{y^{-3/2}}{z^{1/2}} \right) \hat{k} \\
&= 15 \hat{\imath} + \frac{3}{2} \sqrt{\frac{z}{y^5}} \hat{j} - \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{\frac{1}{y^3 z}} \hat{k}
\end{align}
$$
