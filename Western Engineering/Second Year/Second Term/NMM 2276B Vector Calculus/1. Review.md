### Examples of Vectors

Figure 1 below depicts a [[Vector]], as a directed line segment. It is “directed” as opposed to just a line segment, because there is a direction (from A to B, and this is communicated by the arrow, which has a beginning and an end). We all recognize this as a vector, no problem.

![[Pasted image 20250108071907.png]]

Vectors may be represented in several ways including an underscore $\underset{\_}{AB}$. Most commonly, vectors are written using the arrow "->" notation. For example: $\overset{->}{AB}$. This represents point A to point B.

**Note:** Regardless you must have different notations for [[Vector|Vectors]] and [[Scalar]].
