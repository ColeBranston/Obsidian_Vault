Almost all of these properties are simple extension (or repeats) of standard properties of 1D integrals.

$$
\text{Linearity:}
$$

$$
\iint_R [a \cdot f(x, y) \pm b \cdot g(x, y)] \, dA = a \iint_R f(x, y) \, dA \pm b \iint_R g(x, y) \, dA
$$

$$
\text{Summation (Union):}
$$

$$
\iint_R f(x, y) \, dA = \iint_{R1} f(x, y) \, dA + \iint_{R2} f(x, y) \, dA \quad \text{where} \, R = R1 \cup R2
$$

$$\text{where R1 and R2 are non-overlapping subregions of R, and} \;R=R_1\cup R_2$$
