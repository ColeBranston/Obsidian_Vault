
>[!User-Client-Developer-Manager]
>Consider a machine that distributes tickets for a train. Travelers have the option of selecting a ticket for a single trip, for multiple trips, or a timecard for a day or a week. The ticket distributor computes the price of the requested ticket based on the area in which the trip will take place and whether the traveler of a child or an adult. 

- In this example, travelers purchasing tickets are **end users**. The train company, contracting the development of the ticket distributor is **the client** of the project. The engineers Realizing the system (and the software), John, Marc, and Zoe, are **developers**. Their boss, Alice, coordinating the work and the communication with the client, is the **project manager**
