If *X* is a binomial random variable with parameters *p* and *n*, then

- the **mean** of X is:

$$\mu=E(X) = np$$

- the **variance** of *X* is:

$$\sigma^2 = V(X) = np(1-p)$$

*A little trick*

These quantities can be easily derived by summing *n* **Bernoulli random variables** and using the definitions of the mean and variance of discrete random variables.
