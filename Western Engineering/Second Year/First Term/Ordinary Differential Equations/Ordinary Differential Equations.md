###### Learning Outcomes
- Solve first order differential equations
- Solve homogeneous and inhomogeneous second order linear differential equations
- Understand where differential equations arise in engineering
- Be able to solve initial value problems using Laplace transforms
- Represent functions by Fourier series
- Understand the theory and applications of ODEs
