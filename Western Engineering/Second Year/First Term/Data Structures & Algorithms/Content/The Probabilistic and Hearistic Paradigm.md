
- Probabilistic algorithms are those that make some choices randomly (or pseudo-randomly; for some problems, it can in fact be proven that the fastest solutions must involve some randomness. 

- Genetic algorithms attempt to find solutions to problems by mimicking biological evolutionary process, with a cycle of random mutations yielding successive generations of "solutions".

- Heuristic algorithms, whose general purpose is not to find an optimal solution, but an approximate solution where the time or resources are limited. They are not practical to find perfect solutions. An example of this would be **local search algorithm**.
