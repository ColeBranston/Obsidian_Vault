 - A stack is a collection of elements, inserted and removed according to the last-in, first-out (LIFO) principle.

- The elements can be inserted or deleted only from one side of the list. The insertion operation is called “push”, and the deletion operation is called “pop”.

- To keep track of the last element in the list, a pointer called “top” is defined.

![[Pasted image 20240919164512.png]]

- In this case, the following design ideas are considered:

	1. The element at the last index of the array is the top of the stack.
	2. Push-Pop operations will always take place at the top of stack.
