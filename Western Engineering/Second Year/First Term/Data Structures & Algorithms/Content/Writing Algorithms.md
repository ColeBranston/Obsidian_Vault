Algorithms can be illustrated by one of the following ways:
- Pseudo-code
	- Kind of structured English that describes all the necessary steps in the task

- Flow-Charts (For Methods), UML (Unified Modeling language) diagrams (Object oriented programming)
	- Provide graphical representation of all the possible steps in a task

- Natural Language
	- The language that is spoken, signed, or written by human beings for general-purpose communication (rarely used for complex or technical algorithms )
