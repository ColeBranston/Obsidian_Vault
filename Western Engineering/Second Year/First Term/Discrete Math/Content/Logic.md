Mathematical logic has to do with truth. Not so much with what is truth but with how to preserve truth. Statements are assertions that can be given a truth value of $truth = \top=1$ or $false = \bot =0$, (but not both).

The following are statements:

- Today is Friday.
- Alice Munro, the disgraced Nobel laurate, attended UWO (1).
- 2+3=5 (1)
- 4 is a prime number (0)

The following are not statements:
- After we die we will all burn in hell.
- What a beautiful day!
- This sentence is a lie (Liar's Paradox),
- *p* is a prime number. (Depends on the value of *p*.)
