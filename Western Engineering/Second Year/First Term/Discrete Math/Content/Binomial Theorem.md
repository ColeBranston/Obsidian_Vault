For $n \geq2$, and $x,y$ numbers (or polynomial variables):

$$(x+y)^n = \sum ^n _{k = 0} x^ky^{n-k}$$
###### Example

In general, we could expand $(x+y)^n =$ sum of words of size *n* in letters *x* and *y*

So, the coefficient of $x^ky^{n-k}$ is the number of words of size *n* with *k* *x's* and *n-k* y's:

$$\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} = {n \choose k}$$ $\therefore (x+y)^4 = (x+y)(x+y)(x+y)(x+y)$
$\therefore (x+y)^4 = {4 \choose 0}y^4 + {4 \choose 1}y^3x +{4 \choose 2}y^2x^2 + {4 \choose 3} x^3 +{4 \choose 4}x^4$
$\therefore (x+y)^4 = y^4 + 4y^3x +6y^2x^2+4x^3 + x^4$
