
- Spark builds a **logical plan** of DataFrame transformations (unexecuted). When you urn an action, Spark's **Catalyst Optimizer**:
	1. Converts the logical plan to an optimized logical plan (predicate pushdown, projection pruning, constant folding).
	2. Converts to a physical plan (chooses operations, join strategies, partition strategies).
	3. Executes tasks across executors.

- The **DAG** is a sequence of stages. A stage is a set of transformations that can be pipelined without shuffle. A **shuffle** (wide dependency) forces a new stage (eg. `groupBy`, `reduceByKey`, `join`).

- **Lazy evaluation** means you can chain many transformations without running any computation until you call and action - this allows Spark to optimize the entire pipeline at once.
