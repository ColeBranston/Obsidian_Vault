Main build files within a SpringBoot project is **pom.xml** for ***Maven*** and **gradle.build** for ***Gradle***.
_________
Summary: 

In summary, Maven is known for its simplicity and convention, making it easy for developers to understand and manage Java projects. Gradle, on the other hand, provides more flexibility and performance optimizations, making it suitable for larger and more complex builds. The choice between Maven and Gradle often comes down to the specific needs and preferences of the project and the development team
