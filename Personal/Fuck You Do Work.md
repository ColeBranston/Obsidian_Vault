
**Study Tips**

1. Take random breaks of 10 seconds to maintain focus.
2.  Stair at a wall for 60 seconds to get into focus before activity.
3. Regulate work intervals to 1 hours and 30 minutes max. 
4. After studying, take a rest or meditate for max of 20 minutes (NSDR)
5. Make sure you get sleep

**If Tired**

1. Wash your face with cold water.
2. Exercise.
3. Put yourself under stress.
